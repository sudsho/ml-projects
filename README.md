| [MCMC from Scratch: Metropolis-Hastings, HMC and the Diagnostics](./mcmc-from-scratch/) | Where the particle filter project points - it ended with an unbiased likelihood estimator, which is what pseudo-marginal MCMC consumes, and that is only worth having if the MCMC half is understood against targets whose answers are known. It turned into a project about the diagnostics rather than the samplers, because every day produced a number that is supposed to report how the sampler is doing and does not: the ESS estimator scores a fully stuck chain as the most efficient setting on the grid, the acceptance rate climbs as conditioning collapses the mixing and peaks at the worst trajectory length in the sweep, the sharpest available HMC identity is biased toward "looks fine" in exactly the regime it exists to detect, and on Neal's funnel the marginal variance lands within 8% of the true 9.00 on a chain that never gets past 0.9 sigma into the neck. R-hat reads 1.0041 with 42 divergences, 1.0000 on four chains agreeing about the wrong distribution, and sqrt((n-1)/n) on four frozen at one point, because it tests agreement rather than correctness. The one diagnostic that tracks the difficulty is the divergence count - 0, 21, 113, 4204 as the neck deepens - and it is the only one computed from where the chain failed to go | ✅ Complete |
| [Particle Filtering for Nonlinear State-Space Models](./particle-filter-nonlinear/) | Where the Kalman project stops. Sequential Monte Carlo from scratch in NumPy on a model whose observation is even in the state, so the posterior is genuinely bimodal and no Gaussian belief can represent it. The posterior mean lands at 2.7e-20 of the peak density and is still the better point estimate; three of four expectations about resampling are wrong, including that the 104x ordering effect is worth nothing over a hundred steps; the even observation turns out not to be what hurts the EKF and UKF, the Jensen gap in the prediction is; backward smoothing is worth 66% and a 12x sweep of filter variance shows that is about the kind of uncertainty rather than the amount; and the likelihood estimator is unbiased at every N while having twice the standard deviation of the thing it estimates | ✅ Complete |
| [Epistemic Uncertainty: Deep Ensembles, MC Dropout and Laplace](./epistemic-uncertainty-ensembles/) | The other half of the uncertainty the MDN project did not model - a density head reports the spread of the data and reports it just as confidently where there is no data at all. Deep ensembles, MC dropout and a last-layer Laplace approximation from scratch in PyTorch, split into aleatoric and epistemic components on a regression problem with a hole punched in its input range, and scored on whether the epistemic part actually grows where the training data stops, ending with two proper scoring rules that rank the four methods in opposite orders | ✅ Complete |
| [Mixture Density Networks for Multimodal Regression](./mixture-density-networks/) | An MDN from scratch in PyTorch - a network emitting Gaussian mixture parameters instead of a point estimate, trained by NLL, on an inverse problem whose conditional is genuinely multimodal. The point estimate returns a legitimate preimage on the most probable branch, looks correct under every point metric, and silently discards 56% of the conditional mass; logsumexp turns out to fix outlier points rather than dying components; component death is settled within 25 epochs and is an initialization event; and pooled PIT calibration passes cleanly while both halves of the input range fail it in opposite directions | ✅ Complete |
